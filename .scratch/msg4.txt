vision: random-crop and random-horizontal-flip on the device (#152)

The CIFAR-10 augmentation pair over the ops the library already has: the
crop pads with zeros through narrow and copy! and cuts per-image windows,
the flip mirrors the width and selects per image with where. One draw-seed
from the torch generator seeds a Racket generator per batch, so a seeded
loader replays its augmentation while the images never leave the device;
the draws are the transforms' own, not torchvision's. Manual section and
AGENTS rosters for tranche 6.
