review: no zero dimension anywhere, and a range that quantizes

The pixel check looked at the last two dimensions, so `[N 0 H W]` passed
and `image-grid` built a grid with no channels, which the manual already
said could not happen. Both contracts ask the whole shape now, which is
also shorter than the helper it replaces.

`fill-value/c` let two values through that the dtype cannot hold: an
int64 fill of 2^63, exactly representable as a double but past the
signed range, reached the native conversion instead of the contract;
and a boolean fill of 0.5 became true. Both are refused, as uint8's
already were.

`value-range/c` took an infinity, which made the scale zero and wrote
every pixel black. `rational?` in place of `real?` takes the infinities
and NaN out together.
