codegen: tranche 6, the classic vision op closure (#152)

batch_norm, leaky_relu, binary_cross_entropy_with_logits, huber_loss,
l1_loss and flip join the allowlist; every one fits the existing kinds, so
the generator is untouched. batch_norm's running statistics are optional
tensors that ATen updates in place when training, which is what lets a
Buffer field carry nn.BatchNorm2d's semantics without a Racket-side write.

Goldens in generated_tranche6_test.cpp pin the per-channel normalisation,
the momentum update of the running statistics (unbiased batch variance),
the eval-mode refusal without statistics, log 2 for BCE at zero logits,
the Huber knee, and both flip forms; C pins for the six signatures; parity
recipes with training, weighted, sum, none and multi-dim drives.
