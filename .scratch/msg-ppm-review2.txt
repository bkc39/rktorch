review: build the grid under no-grad, and name the pad value's contract

`make_grid` is decorated with `no_grad`; without it every `copy!` here
extended the caller's graph into what is only a picture of the batch.
The body runs under `with-no-grad` now. The one-image path still hands
back the batch's own image, which torch leaves requiring grad even under
`no_grad`, as it does there.

`#:pad-value` took any real and let the nested `full` refuse it, so a
uint8 batch with -1 was `full: contract violation` blaming
torch/vision/ppm.rkt for what the caller passed. `creation-ops.rkt` now
exports `(fill-value/c dtype)`, built from the table `full`'s own
`#:pre/desc` uses, and `image-grid` states it with `->i`, so the
violation names `image-grid` and `uint8-fill-value`.

`image/c` requires a positive height and width: a PPM header states
both, and a zero in either wrote a file no reader accepts.
