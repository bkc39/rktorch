nn: BatchNorm2d, BatchNorm1d and the vision losses (#152)

The first Buffer customers: running-mean, running-var and an int64
num-batches-tracked, with PyTorch's init (ones, zeros, zeros, ones, 0),
updated by ATen in 'train mode and read in 'eval; the counter is bumped
through an int64 cast because add promotes it. Both layers share one
normalising helper and differ in the rank they accept. The state dict
carries the buffers, so a trained BatchNorm round-trips through
safetensors, counter included.

binary-cross-entropy-with-logits (#:weight, #:pos-weight), huber-loss
(#:delta) and l1-loss join loss.rkt with the mean reduction of their
siblings. Python twin batch_norm_forward.py pins the training forward, the
statistics it leaves behind, the counter and the eval forward.
