c_api: the half pair, raw element bytes, and autocast (#152)

TR_DTYPE_FLOAT16 and TR_DTYPE_BFLOAT16 join the enum after uint8, so the
earlier values stay ABI; the two mappings gain their cases and every
constructor and conversion inherits them, with values reading back through
the float32 copy path as before. tr_tensor_copy_bytes and tr_from_bytes
move a tensor's element bytes in its own dtype, the safetensors payload and
the only way a 16-bit float leaves the process without widening.
tr_set_autocast_enabled / tr_is_autocast_enabled / tr_autocast_dtype wrap
at::autocast per device type, the shape of the grad-mode pair: enabling
sets the device's cast dtype, which must be one of the half pair, and
disabling drops the cast cache as torch.autocast's exit does.

Gtests: the half casts and their float32 readback, the byte round trip for
all seven dtypes with IEEE bit patterns pinned, and CPU bfloat16 autocast
casting a matmul until disabled; pins for the five signatures.
