vision: ResNet on CIFAR-10, the classic supervised recipe (#152 leg 3)

torch/vision/resnet.rkt: BasicBlock (two 3x3 convolutions under batch
norm, a projection shortcut only where the shape changes and no field to
name otherwise, as torchvision) and ResNet (a 3x3 stem for 32x32 images,
four stages of blocks doubling the width, global average pooling and a
linear head; ResNet-18 at base 64 by default, 11.2M parameters). Conv2d
takes #:bias? so the convolutions under batch norm draw no bias, as
PyTorch's bias=False.

examples/racket/09-resnet.rkt: the loop with the three new ingredients,
random-crop and random-horizontal-flip on the device from the loader's
generator, SGD with Nesterov momentum and weight decay under one-cycle-lr
stepped per batch, and the forward under with-autocast on CUDA with the
backward outside; run-example is the seeded float32 core on the fixture
(the narrow base-16 net, five full-batch steps) that the harness and the
twin drive; train-cifar10 is the headline run with the test accuracy after
every epoch. Twin 09_resnet.py; harness with the parameter tree pinned;
manual section; AGENTS rosters.
