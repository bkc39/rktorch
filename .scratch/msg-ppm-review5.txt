review: quantize off the graph, and check the span not just its ends

`write-ppm` built an autograd chain through sub, mul, add and clamp when
handed a tensor that required grad. The uint8 cast severs the result,
but the intermediates hold their native graph until a collection, which
is what `save_image` avoids by running under no-grad; the quantization
does the same now.

`value-range/c` asked its endpoints to be finite but not their
difference, so `'(-1e308 1e308)` still made the scale zero. It checks
the span the implementation actually divides by, which also refuses a
pair so close together that the span underflows.

The comment over `one-image` said what vision.scrbl says.
