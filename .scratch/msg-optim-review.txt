review: cache the scheduler's rate, and five smaller fixes

`scheduler-rate` recomputed the rate on every call, so reading it
advanced a stateful `lambda-lr` callback and could report a value the
optimizer never saw; it now returns what `apply-rate!` last wrote, as
`get_last_lr()` does. The rest come from the same review:

- `one-cycle-lr` with `#:pct-start 1` put the peak at the last step and
  divided the descent by zero, installing a NaN rate; the contract
  excludes 1.
- `lambda-lr` took any arity-1 procedure while the manual promised
  `(-> exact-nonnegative-integer? real?)`; it takes that now, so a
  callback returning a non-real is caller blame, not a tensor error.
- `#:weight-decay` accepted negatives on all three optimizers, quietly
  turning regularization around. `torch.optim` rejects them, and so do
  these.
- `optimizer?` reaches the facade through a `checked` submodule, the
  plain/checked split every other shared name in torch/nn uses.
- Two comments in scheduler.rkt repeated nn.scrbl's prose.
