vision: write-ppm takes a floating-point or uint8 image

An int64 image holding 0 to 255 went through the range transform under
the default `'(0 1)` and quantized to white, silently. The range keyword
is the control for that, but the owner's call is to state the dtypes
instead, so `image/c` names them.

The list carries float16 and bfloat16, which this branch has no way to
make: #165 adds them in the same arc, and a sample tensor out of an
autocast forward would otherwise stop writing the moment that lands.
