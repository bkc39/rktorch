nn: a forward formal states the shape it takes, as a contract (#152)

BatchNorm checked its input's rank with an unless guard inside the
forward. AGENTS.md puts that check in the signature, but define-layer had
nowhere to put it: #:contract reaches the constructor alone, and a private
helper cannot carry one. So a forward formal may now be written
[x : image-batch/c], the notation #99 proposes for #:init, and the rank
BatchNorm2d and BatchNorm1d accept is part of their signature.

The checker is built once where the layer is defined, not per call, so a
forward pays one flat check. The violation names the layer and the
contract, as a constructor's does, and the arity error of #18 is
untouched. A bare formal accepts anything, so every other layer is
unchanged. feature-batch/c ([N C] or [N C L]) joins image-batch/c in
foreign/contracts.rkt.
