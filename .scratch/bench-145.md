From #165 (half precision): the DDPM UNet at batch 128 under bfloat16 autocast peaks at 9008 MiB against 9714 MiB in float32, 7% less, since the parameters, gradients, Adam moments and GroupNorm stay float32 and the weight-cast cache holds a bfloat16 copy of every weight for the extent; batch 256 fits at 16012 MiB with 313 ms/step, and per step autocast is 1.4x faster than float32 at the same batch. So autocast is a throughput lever more than a memory one here; the ledger's pressure-driven collection remains the memory fix.
