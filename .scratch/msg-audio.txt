audio test: one temp file per round-trip case

The four parameters differ only by extension and case, and they all
wrote testing::TempDir() + the name. On macOS, where the filesystem
is case-insensitive, rt.flac and rt.FLAC are one file; ctest starts
the two within 40 ms of each other, so whichever ran second read
what the first was rewriting or had already removed:

  audio_test.cpp:55: Failure
  tr_audio_info: cannot open .../rt.FLAC: Format not recognised.

Distinct stems keep the coverage of both extension cases and take
the race away.
