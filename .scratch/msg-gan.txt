examples: a DCGAN and a VAE on MNIST, with sample grids (#152 leg 4)

10-dcgan.rkt: the DCGAN shrunk to 28x28, a generator of one linear layer
and two transposed convolutions under batch norm with a tanh output, a
discriminator of two strided convolutions under leaky ReLU and batch norm
reading one logit; one step trains the discriminator on the real batch
against ones and the detached fake batch against zeros, then the generator
on the same fake batch against ones, with binary-cross-entropy-with-logits
and two adams at 2e-4 with #:beta1 0.5, the latent drawn on the CPU so a
seeded run replays on the GPU as the twin draws it.

11-vae.rkt: the linear VAE of the PyTorch examples, 784-400-20, whose
forward takes the reparameterization noise from the caller and returns the
logits, the means and the log-variances; the loss is the reference's
reconstruction cross-entropy plus KL, divided by the batch; decode maps
latents to logits for sampling.

Both headline runs write a 10x10 grid per epoch from one fixed latent draw
through image-grid and write-ppm. Twins 10_dcgan.py and 11_vae.py train
the fixture three and five full-batch steps; harnesses pin the parameter
trees and shapes; timeouts; AGENTS roster.
